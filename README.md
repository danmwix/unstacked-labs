# unstacked-labs
🧠 AI Vaccination & Child Health Tracker

A practical AI-powered system designed to help parents track their children’s immunization schedules with ease. The system allows parents to register their child once and then automatically receive timely reminders for upcoming vaccines. It also includes an AI assistant built using Google Genkit and Vertex AI, which provides health tips, age-based guidance, and answers to common child-health questions in a conversational format.

🚀 Project Overview

In many parts of Kenya, parents rely on physical clinic booklets to monitor vaccination schedules. These booklets are often misplaced, damaged, or forgotten, leading to missed vaccines and delayed immunization. This project offers a simple, accessible, and intelligent solution that helps parents stay on track with their child’s immunization needs.

The system works by:

Registering each child once

Automatically calculating their vaccine schedule using official immunization timelines

Generating reminders when vaccines are due

Providing real-time answers and health tips through an AI assistant

Supporting multiple children under one parent account

The goal is to make childhood vaccination tracking easy, reliable, and accessible even for caregivers with limited digital skills.

🤖 AI Capabilities (Genkit + Vertex AI)

The project integrates an AI assistant powered by Google Genkit and Vertex AI to enhance user interaction. The AI can:

Answer questions about upcoming vaccines

Provide nutritional advice based on the child’s age

Explain danger signs and when to seek medical help

Offer simple explanations of vaccine purpose and benefits

Guide first-time parents with day-to-day child health care

This transforms the tracker from a simple reminder tool into a digital child-health companion.

🔧 Key Features

Child Registration: Parents enter basic child details (name, DOB, gender).

Automated Vaccine Timeline: The system calculates vaccine due dates based on the child’s date of birth.

Smart Reminders: The system sends reminders when a vaccine date is approaching.

AI Health Assistant: A conversational assistant that gives childcare guidance using Genkit + Vertex AI.

Multiple Child Support: Parents can manage more than one child effortlessly.

User-Friendly Interface: Designed to be simple for all users, including non-technical caregivers.

🧩 System Workflow

Parent registers a child

System generates the complete vaccine schedule

Upcoming due dates are monitored automatically

Reminders are generated

Parent interacts with the AI assistant whenever they need additional help or guidance

This workflow ensures a smooth experience from registration to vaccination follow-up.
